# Resources for students with a disability

If you are an RMIT student with a disability, the library is here to help, with a dedicated librarian and multiple resources.

- Text digitisation

- Helping to obtain digital resources

- Converting documents from PDF to text

## Wheelchair access

Wheelchair access is available in our Saigon, Hanoi and Danang locations.

Access to the Beanland library is via lifts in Building 1, the Beanland Building.

Within the Library, Level 3 is accessible via wheelchair

Toilets are located near the entrance to The Beanland Library.

## Equitable Learning and Accessibility

RMIT University Vietnam Library liaises with ELA (Equitable Learning & Accessibility) to support people with disabilities by providing resources in appropriate formats.
