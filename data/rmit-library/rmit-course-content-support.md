# Develop course content

Lecturers are provided with a wide range of credible academic resources and expert support in sourcing, scanning, linking and embedding these in course content.

## The eReserve service

Does your course material need to be available online? Please send your Liaison Librarian the list and we will help you make it accessible.

## Reading list assistance

The library will help you to integrate reading materials with library resources, as well as track student activity on the reading material.

## Library collections

The RMIT library collections are sorted by format or interest.

## Add library resources to Canvas

This guide provides information on linking and embedding Library resources in your Canvas course and ensuring copyright compliance.

## Purchasing books

The library purchases print and e-books to support teaching, learning and research. You can suggest a title for purchase to your Liaison Librarian, or fill in an online form.
