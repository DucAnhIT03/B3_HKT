# Workshops and consultations for students

### The library offers a wide range of library workshops to teach students research skills for general research purposes and for specific assignment requirements.

## Tailored workshops for specific courses

The library offers research skills workshops for selected courses in the following programs:

- Commerce, Management

- Economics, Finance, Marketing, Accountancy

- Professional Communication, Design, Fashion

- IT, Engineering

- MBA

- English

Schedule and registration information of the tailored workshop are available via your liaison librarians .

### Library tutorials

Watch videos on how to use library resources and services. (External link to YouTube)

## Research consultations

Set up with an appointment with a liaison librarian when you or your students need:

- Research advice for a student's research assignment

- Instruction on how to search the library online databases

- Instruction on how to evaluate and reference your sources

### Set up a research consultation

You can book an appointment with a librarian at the service desk or contact us via email or phone.
