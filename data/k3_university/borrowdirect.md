# BorrowDirect

BorrowDirect lets Harvard students, faculty and staff request items from partner libraries for free delivery to Harvard when the item is unavailable through HOLLIS.

## Delivery and loan rules

Available material normally arrives within four business days and must be collected in person. The borrower is notified by email. A BorrowDirect loan lasts 16 weeks and cannot be renewed. Pending requests appear in the BorrowDirect account, while checked-out items appear in HOLLIS. BorrowDirect material may be returned to any Harvard library.

## Eligible material

The service covers printed books and music scores that the owning library is willing to lend. It does not supply books already available through Harvard Library; books designated by the owner as reference, reserve or rare; bound journals; or journal articles.

Harvard students and faculty may also visit a partner library and check out books using a Harvard ID and BorrowDirect account. Material borrowed in person from a partner must be returned to that owning library rather than to Harvard Library.
