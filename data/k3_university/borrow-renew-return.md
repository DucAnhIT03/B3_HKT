# Borrow, Renew, and Return Library Materials

## Request and borrow

Use HOLLIS, the Harvard Library catalog, to find and request books, articles, media, and other materials. Book pickup requests are normally filled in one to four business days. Scan & Deliver can send a scan of a journal article or book chapter by email. Interlibrary Loan can be used when the required material is not available at Harvard.

The Library also lends equipment including headphones, laptop chargers, tripods, SD cards, 360-degree cameras and virtual-reality equipment.

## Loan periods, renewals and returns

Regular materials are generally lent for a semester, although the item record in HOLLIS gives the specific loan period and technology loans are shorter. If no other user requests a regular loan item, its loan period is automatically renewed up to five times. Circulating material may be returned to any Harvard library. Regular loans can be recalled at the Library's discretion, with notice sent by email.

## Fines and fees

There is no standard overdue fine. Recalled material costs $3 per day, up to $45 per item. Reserve materials and other short-term loans cost $3 per hour when late. The standard replacement fee for a lost or long-overdue item is $100, but the amount can vary by item and holding library.
