# Librarians for First-Year Students

Harvard assigns dedicated First-Year Librarians to help first-year students use the Library.

## Support available

First-Year Librarians serve as personal library contacts for first-year students. They provide guidance, consultations and referrals while students begin exploring research interests and writing research papers for their classes.

Students who need subject-specific help can also contact a Library Liaison. Research Consultations provide personalized guidance from a library specialist either online or in person.
