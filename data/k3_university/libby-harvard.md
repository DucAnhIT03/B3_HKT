# Libby for Harvard

Libby by OverDrive provides popular ebooks and audiobooks from Harvard Library's Farnsworth collection. Access is available to current Harvard students, faculty and staff.

## Getting started

Install the Libby app, open its menu, choose **Add Library**, search for Harvard University, and sign in with HarvardKey. Desktop users can instead visit `libbyapp.com/library/harvardu`.

## Borrowing limits

A Libby loan lasts 14 days. A user may borrow no more than three books at once and may place up to five books on hold.

Users can search for a title with the magnifying-glass icon or browse with the building icon. A requested addition to the Farnsworth collection should be submitted as a Purchase Request with **Online** selected as the format and **Farnsworth Room** selected as the library.
