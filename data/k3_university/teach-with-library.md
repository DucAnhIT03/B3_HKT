# Teach with the Library

Harvard Library provides course support to help students navigate the Library and find the materials they need.

## General library instruction

Librarians can create an instruction session for a particular class or topic. A session can take place in a library space, in the class's usual room, or remotely. Faculty can arrange it by contacting their library liaison or submitting a request.

Sessions can be adapted to the course's skill level and discipline. Topics include primary sources, college-level research, literature reviews, oral history and interviews, evaluating information, database searching, and the interpretation of maps.

## Special collections and archives

Participating special-collections libraries and archives offer class visits ranging from a survey of material selected by faculty to a guided exploration designed by library staff. They can also support assignments that require students to engage with the collections.

Faculty may request one class visit or several visits for deeper study of archives, manuscripts, maps and rare books. Requests should be submitted as early as possible so staff and space are available; a librarian or archivist will then help plan the session.
